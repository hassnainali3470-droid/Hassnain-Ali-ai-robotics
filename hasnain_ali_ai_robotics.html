01 / Introduction

What are AI and Robotics?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) helps computers recognize patterns, make predictions, and support decisions. Robotics combines mechanical design, electronics, sensors, and software to build machines that interact with the physical world. Together, they can create robots that perceive their surroundings and respond intelligently.

🧠

Artificial Intelligence

Algorithms that learn from data and perform tasks such as image recognition, language processing, and planning.

🤖

Robotics

Machines that use sensors, motors, and control software to perform physical actions.

⚙️

Intelligent Automation

Systems that combine software, data, and hardware to carry out repeatable tasks with less manual intervention.

03 / How it works

From sensors to smart actions

Sense

Cameras, microphones, and other sensors collect information from the environment.

Process

Software cleans and interprets sensor data to identify useful signals.

Decide

Rules, models, or planning algorithms select an appropriate action.

Act and improve

Motors or software execute the action; feedback helps adjust future behavior.

04 / Learning roadmap

Learn AI & Robotics step by step

Step 1 — Learn Python

Practice variables, loops, functions, and small coding projects.

Step 2 — Build math foundations

Study algebra, basic calculus, probability, and linear algebra.

Step 3 — Explore machine learning

Learn about datasets, training, testing, and evaluating models.

Step 4 — Understand electronics

Experiment with sensors, motors, and a beginner microcontroller.

Step 5 — Create a robotics project

Build a line-following robot, obstacle detector, or simulated robot.

Step 6 — Document and share

Write about your project, show results, and explain what you learned.
